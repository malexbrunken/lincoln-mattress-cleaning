## Why the count stops

NWU's counting season follows the plants. Bolick's 2001 State Museum note says the museum's Division of Botany counted pollen five days a week from late February to mid-October, and NWU's count keeps the same rhythm today. LLCHD's air quality page says the department doesn't conduct seasonal pollen counts and links to NWU instead.
