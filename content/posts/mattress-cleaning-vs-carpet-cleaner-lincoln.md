Carpet cleaners do good work on carpet, and their equipment is built for floors. A mattress is foam, latex or fiber batting under a quilted cover, and water that goes into it is slow to come back out. That is why we start from the bed: identify the materials, set the steam and pass speed, work the seams, tufts and edges, then HEPA vacuum and run UV-C light treatment. The bed stays unmade until it is dry to the touch, and we run a moisture check before we leave.

## Why does moisture matter in a Lincoln bedroom?

Lincoln Airport's NOAA normals put the average July dew point near 65°F, and the Lincoln-Lancaster County Health Department recommends 30 to 50 percent indoor humidity. Our [Lincoln humidity and bed mites guide](/allergy-season/lincoln-humidity-and-bed-mites) has the month-by-month numbers. A low-moisture method keeps the mattress out of that fight.

## When is a carpet cleaner the better hire?

When the job is floors. If you need wall-to-wall carpet, area rugs or a sofa cleaned, call a carpet or upholstery company. We don't do those, and we would rather say so than stretch our equipment past the bed.

## What should you ask before you book anyone?

- Is the mattress its own appointment, or an add-on to the floors?
- What equipment touches the mattress, and how much water goes in?
- Do you treat the seams, tufts and edges, or only the top panel?
- What is included in the price, and is urine or pet odor extra?
- How do you confirm the mattress is dry to the touch before the bed is made?
