Bed mites (house dust mites) are a fact of indoor life, not a sign of a dirty house. Our dry vapor steam uses heat to kill them; in published tests, a 96°C steam cleaner killed all of the mites in treated carpet and mattress samples (Glass and Needham, 2004). The rest of this guide covers what that does and does not mean.

## What bed mites actually are

They are microscopic animals that feed on shed skin flakes. They need warmth and humidity, and a mattress provides both: warmed by a sleeper for eight hours a night, humidified by the same person, and rarely cleaned because the problem is invisible.

## Why winter in Lincoln matters

From October through April, a Lincoln bedroom is a closed room with the heat running and the windows shut. Warmth, humidity from the sleeper and a food source, for months at a time, is the environment bed mites do well in.

## What actually helps

**Heat.** Steam heat killed bed mites in published tests: Glass and Needham (2004) reported 100 percent mortality of D. farinae in carpet and mattress samples treated with a 96°C steam cleaner, and Colloff (1995) found no live mites in steam-treated, mite-seeded carpet squares over four months. Those were study conditions with those machines, not a measurement in your bedroom. Sleep Sanitation's Knowledge Center covers both studies in detail in [Does steam kill dust mites in mattresses?](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-dust-mites-allergens/does-steam-kill-dust-mites-in-mattresses)

**HEPA vacuuming.** Dead mites and droppings stay in the fabric until something lifts them, which is why every visit includes it.

**Lower humidity.** A drier bedroom is less hospitable to bed mites. Our [bedroom CO₂ testing](/services/co2-bedroom-testing) is a separate service for households who want to know how well a closed bedroom ventilates overnight.

**Encasements, fitted afterwards.** Fit an encasement *after* treatment, not before, so you are not sealing the debris inside it.

**Washing bedding hot.** Sheets, pillowcases and washable covers on a hot cycle in your own machine.

## How long does it last?

It depends on your room, not on our visit. Bed mites return from the carpet, upholstery and bedding around the mattress, at a rate set by humidity, pets, bedding habits and whether an encasement is used. Anyone promising a permanent result is selling something they cannot deliver.
