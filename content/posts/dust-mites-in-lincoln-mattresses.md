If you wake up congested in a bedroom that feels fine by mid-morning, the mattress is a more useful suspect than the season. Dust mites are the most common reason, and the good news is that the fix is mechanical rather than medical.

## What dust mites actually are

They are microscopic organisms that feed on shed skin cells. They need warmth and humidity, both of which a mattress provides in abundance: warmed by the sleeper for eight hours a night, humidified by the same person, rarely cleaned because it is not visible.

They are not a sign that your house is dirty. Every home has them. Allergy and indoor-air guidance typically points at bedding and mattresses as a major indoor exposure site because of how much time you spend there and how close your airway is to the surface.

The allergen is not the mite itself so much as the waste they produce. That is what accumulates in quilting, seams, and fiber batting.

## Why Lincoln households get hit harder

From October through April, most bedrooms here are closed rooms with the heat running and windows shut. That is the ideal dust-mite environment, sustained for months at a time. A humidified, warmed, sealed box with a food source and no air exchange.

Add older housing stock, a lot of pets, and — around UNL — mattresses with multi-tenant histories, and the reservoir builds faster than most people assume.

## What actually helps

**Heat.** Dry vapor steam neutralizes existing populations and their waste in the upper layers without introducing moisture. This is the point where most cleaning methods go wrong: adding water to a mattress to solve a humidity-loving organism is working against yourself.

**Low humidity.** Keeping bedroom humidity under roughly 50% makes the room markedly less hospitable. That is why our [72-hour CO₂ and air-quality testing](/services/co2-bedroom-testing) often turns up ventilation problems that nobody had thought to look for.

**Encasements, fitted afterwards.** An allergen-proof encasement materially extends the benefit — but fit it *after* treatment, not before, so you are not sealing a reservoir inside it.

**Washing bedding hot.** Sheets, pillowcases, and washable covers on a hot cycle in your own machine is more effective than anything we can do on site.

## How long does it last?

Honestly: it depends on your environment, not on our visit.

In a typical Lincoln home with standard HVAC filtration and normal hygiene habits, we describe the benefit as lasting roughly **12–18 months**. Households with pets or significant allergy sensitivity generally do better on a semi-annual schedule. Reinfestation is a function of humidity, bedding hygiene, pets, and whether an encasement is used.

Anyone promising you a permanent result is selling something they cannot deliver.

## What we will not claim

We do not treat allergies or asthma. What a thorough mattress sanitation appointment does is remove a large, concentrated, nightly exposure source from the room where you spend a third of your life. Many customers describe easier mornings. The honest framing is that we reduce an exposure source — we do not treat a medical condition, and you should not skip medical advice because you had a mattress cleaned.
