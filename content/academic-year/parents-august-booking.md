## What is the August booking checklist?

1. **Confirm the bed is off campus.** Hall beds are out of scope.
2. **Pick a weekday before furniture arrives.** Leave room for the bed to stay unmade until it is dry to the touch.
3. **Call or text (402) 512-5658** or use [our book page](/book). Lincoln Mattress Cleaning is operated by Sleep Sanitation.
4. **Name stains, pets and used-bed history** so we bring the right enzyme work. Ordinary urine accidents are included in {{price.first}}.
5. **Check the care label and warranty terms** before we start.

## How is this different from the parent's checklist?

The [parent's mattress checklist for the UNL year](/academic-year/parents-checklist) walks January through May. This page is only the August booking call: dates, price and what to say on the phone.

## What does a multi-bed apartment cost?

See [/pricing](/pricing) for current offers. Underside/full-surface treatment is {{price.underside}} per mattress when needed.

## What we don't know

- **Your exact move-in hour.** Buildings vary; give us a window, not a guess.
- **Whether parking will be open near City Campus that week.** Plan for UNL move-in traffic if you are close to Memorial Stadium.
