## Who books a Greek house cleaning?

Usually a house manager, alumni board contact or parent who can meet us at the address. We need a clear path to each mattress and an empty room when possible. Multi-mattress visits use the labeled additional rates: {{price.additionalLarge}} for each additional full, queen or king and {{price.additionalKids}} for each additional kids bed (twin/full) in the same visit.

## When do semester turnovers make sense?

December and May, while members are gone and bedding is stripped. August works if the house is resetting beds before new members arrive. Book ahead of the busy UNL move-in week.

## What about bed mites and odor?

Bed mites (house dust mites) favor closed bedrooms. Steam heat and HEPA vacuuming are part of every visit, followed by UV-C light treatment. Every visit includes two checks: the bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum and a moisture check after the job. 72-hour bedroom CO₂ testing is optional and priced by quote. Science and limits live in Sleep Sanitation's [dust mites and allergens hub](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-dust-mites-allergens).

## What we don't know

- **Each chapter's bed size and who owns each mattress.** Ask the house before you book.
- **Whether a chapter's UAH status changes mid-year.** UNL says the approval list can change; check fsl.unl.edu.
