## What should NWU hall students pack for the bed?

Extra-long sheets, blankets, pillows and a mattress pad. NWU's move-in tips list those as bring items and do not list a personal mattress. Hall beds stay with Residential Education.

## When does an off-campus reset fit Prairie Wolves Welcome?

Book for a weekday before August 19, or for the week of August 21 if returners are the ones moving into an apartment. Leave the bed unmade until it is dry to the touch; we run a moisture check after the job. Check the care label and warranty terms first.

## Bringing a used bed to an NWU apartment?

Inspect it before it goes in the car. See [buying a used mattress for a Lincoln apartment](/academic-year/used-mattress-for-a-lincoln-apartment). Method detail lives in Sleep Sanitation's [used-mattress sanitizing guide](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-owners-decision-center/can-a-used-mattress-be-sanitized).

## What we don't know

- **2027 NWU move-in times.** Residential Education publishes them closer to summer.
- **Whether a specific Townhouse or suite bed has ever been cleaned.** Ask the student who lived there last.
