## When is the best time to clean a mattress before move-in?

1. **Before you carry anything in,** look at the bed in good light. Nebraska Extension in Lancaster County lists the signs of bed bugs as live bugs, dead bugs, shed exoskeletons and black fecal marks, and says to check fabric seams, folds and zippers first.
2. **If you find bugs, get them identified.** Extension reports that about half of the suspected bed bugs brought to Nebraska Extension in Lancaster County turn out to be bat bugs, which are handled differently.
3. **Book cleaning for the empty room,** before your furniture arrives, so the mattress has the room to itself.
4. **Check your care label and warranty terms** before you book any cleaning.
5. **Make the bed only when it is dry to the touch.** The moisture check before we leave tells you where it stands.

## How does UNL move-in week affect scheduling?

UNL ran Move-In 2026 check-in through Memorial Stadium, and residence hall moves filled August 16 to 19. If you are moving into an apartment near City Campus that week, plan around campus traffic, or schedule your own move and any cleaning for the days before.

## What we don't know

- **Your building's move-in date.** Off-campus move-in dates are not set by UNL.
