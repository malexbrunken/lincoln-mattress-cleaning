## What does Woodhaven Hall provide?

Woodhaven Hall is the residence hall on SCC's Lincoln campus at 8800 O Street. The 2026–2027 Residence Life Handbook describes it as suite-style, with double-adjoining rooms connected by a kitchenette, single-adjoining rooms, ADA rooms and a limited number of private singles. Every room comes furnished, including a twin XL bed. Personal mattresses and water beds aren't permitted; a mattress topper is. So pack twin XL sheets, a topper if you want one, and leave the mattress question to Residence Life.

## How is SCC different from the other Lincoln campuses we cover?

SCC runs on a two-weekday check-in, typically the Thursday and Friday before fall classes, with assigned times instead of one big move-in day, and its Lincoln campus sits on the east side of the city at 88th and O Streets rather than near downtown. Our [UNL halls vs off-campus guide](/academic-year/unl-residence-halls-vs-off-campus), [Nebraska Wesleyan move-in](/academic-year/nebraska-wesleyan-move-in) and [Union Adventist move-in](/academic-year/union-adventist-move-in) pages cover the other schools' calendars.

## Where does a mattress cleaning fit for an SCC student?

Off campus and at home. Students who commute or live in an apartment sleep on their own beds, and those are the ones we can clean. Book the week before August 17 or during the break after December 11, when the room is easiest to clear. The visit is a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with two checks included: a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum. The bed stays unmade until it is dry to the touch. Every rate is on our [pricing page](/pricing).
