## Which UAU beds do we clean?

None inside Rees, Prescott or Culver Halls. Those mattresses stay with the university. We clean mattresses in off-campus apartments and houses in Lincoln, including units near South 48th Street.

## How do Rees beds differ from Prescott and Culver?

UAU publishes Rees Hall beds as 36 by 72 inches and Prescott and Culver as 36 by 80 inch extra-long twins. Sheet shopping depends on the hall. That detail matters for packing, not for a hall cleaning we will not book.

## When does an off-campus reset fit Spark Start?

Book a weekday before August 17 while the room is empty. Call or text (402) 512-5658. Check the care label and warranty terms first. How steam works on foam is covered in Sleep Sanitation's [cleaning methods hub](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-cleaning-methods), not on this page.

## What we don't know

- **Whether nextsteps or Spark Start wins if the two pages disagree on August 17 vs 18.** Spark Start is the detailed schedule we follow here; confirm on uau.edu before you travel.
- **2027 Spark Start dates.** UAU publishes them closer to summer.
