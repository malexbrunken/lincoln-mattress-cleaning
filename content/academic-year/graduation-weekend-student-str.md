## Why does graduation weekend matter for mattresses?

Families book near City Campus for commencement, and students turn over apartments the same week halls close. Hosts see back-to-back stays with little time to strip and reset a bed. A weekday cleaning after checkout is the realistic slot.

## What should a host do that week?

1. **Strip the bed at checkout** (or have the emergency contact do it).
2. **Book Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–6 p.m.** Call or text (402) 512-5658.
3. **Leave the bed unmade until it is dry to the touch.** We run a moisture check after the job.
4. **Check the care label and warranty terms** before any cleaning.

City license, guest cap and tax detail stay on [Lincoln short-term rental rules](/airbnb-hosts/lincoln-short-term-rental-rules).

## How is this different from May move-out for students?

[May move-out and disposal](/academic-year/may-move-out-and-disposal) is for students deciding whether a bed stays, moves or goes to the North 48th Street facility. This page is for hosts turning a guest bed during the same calendar crush.

## What we don't know

- **The 2027 commencement and hall-close dates.** UNL publishes them in spring.
- **How many campus-adjacent STRs will be booked that weekend.** Demand varies by year.
