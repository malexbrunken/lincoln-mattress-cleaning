## What does a residence hall student need?

- **Spring:** nothing to buy yet. The housing application opens in January and room assignments follow in May.
- **Summer:** check whether the bed will be lofted. UNL lofts beds in traditional halls and two-bedroom suites, which affects how thick a topper can sensibly be.
- **Move-in:** pack a mattress pad or encasement and washable bedding. Nebraska Extension suggests light-colored sheets or encasements because they make bed bugs easier to spot.
- **Breaks:** UNL halls stay open over fall, Thanksgiving, winter and spring breaks. If your student travels, Extension's advice for returning home is to unpack outside or in the garage and run clothes in the dryer on high heat for 20 to 30 minutes.
- **May:** the move-out checklist says to check the mattress's condition and submit a Fix-It for any damage, and to leave the original mattress pad bed cover.

## What about an off-campus apartment?

1. **When you pick the apartment,** ask whether it comes furnished and how old the bed is, so you know whether to bring one.
2. **On move-in day,** check the bed before the first night. See [our August move-in plan](/academic-year/august-move-in).
3. **If you buy a used bed,** follow [the used mattress inspection](/academic-year/used-mattress-for-a-lincoln-apartment) first.
4. **Book cleaning before your student's furniture arrives,** while the apartment is empty.
5. **In May,** decide early whether the bed is staying, moving or going. [May move-out](/academic-year/may-move-out-and-disposal) lists Lincoln's drop-off for residents.

## What we don't know

- **Your student's building schedule.** Off-campus move-in and move-out dates are not set by UNL.
- **Spring 2027 move-out times.** UNL publishes them closer to May.
