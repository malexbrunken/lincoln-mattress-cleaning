## What is different about a dorm mattress?

Ownership and access. UNL, NWU and Union Adventist provide the hall mattress. Students do not book a professional cleaning on it. Pack bedding that fits the published size (UNL and most NWU first-year halls are extra-long twin; UAU Rees Hall lists 36 by 72).

## What is different about an apartment mattress?

You (or the person who furnished the unit) control it. That is when dry vapor steam, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment help: bed mites (house dust mites), ordinary urine accidents and odor. Every visit includes two checks, and the bed stays unmade until it is dry to the touch.

## When is sanitizing worth it off campus?

- Before the first night on a used or handed-down bed.
- At August move-in while the room is empty.
- Between semesters if the same bed is staying.
- After a pet or spill you can still smell.

When the core is saturated or the cover is torn through, replacement is the honest answer. Sleep Sanitation's [decision guide](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-owners-decision-center/mattress-owners-decision-guide-clean-replace-or-hire) covers that call.

## What we don't know

- **Whether a specific furnished apartment will replace its beds this year.** Ask before you spend.
- **Hall policies that change by school year.** Confirm sizes on each school's housing page.
