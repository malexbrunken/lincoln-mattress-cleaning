## Why the holidays are a turnover crunch

The Ohio State weekend and Thanksgiving are five days apart, and Christmas and New Year's stays run back to back. That leaves few weekdays to clean between groups. Blocking one preparation night in Airbnb gives you a weekday slot; a cleaned mattress is the easiest upgrade to put in your listing photos and reviews before the busiest family-travel weeks.
