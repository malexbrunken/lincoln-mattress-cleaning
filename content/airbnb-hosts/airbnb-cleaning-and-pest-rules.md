## Two rulebooks for a Lincoln listing

A Lincoln short-term rental answers to the city and to Airbnb, and only one of them writes cleaning rules. The City of Lincoln's Building and Safety page covers the $250 license, the guest cap of two per sleeping area, signs, and an emergency contact who can reach the property within 45 minutes. We found nothing on the Lincoln page about cleaning or pests. Airbnb's rules fill that gap for every Lincoln host who lists there.

## What Airbnb's texts say, quoted carefully

- **Ground rules for home hosts.** Its "Health and safety" bullet says listings should be free of health hazards, with mold and pests as examples, and a second cleanliness bullet asks for a high standard of cleanliness. "Guest turnover" says to clean between every stay: laundry, trash, vacuuming or sweeping, wiping down surfaces. For repeated or severe violations, Airbnb says it may suspend a listing or require proof that issues were fixed before hosting resumes.
- **Rebooking and refund policy.** Under it, a Lincoln listing that is "not reasonably clean and sanitary" or contains "safety or health hazards" at check-in is a Reservation Issue; guests must report within 72 hours after discovery, and any refund comes out of the host's payout.
- **The unclean-at-check-in article, number 2993.** The airbnb.com version doesn't mention bed bugs, while the airbnb.ca and airbnb.co.uk versions add a sentence naming "bed bugs, mould or other allergens" as possible immediate health risks. For a Lincoln listing we read the U.S. text as current, and pests fall under the ground rules either way.

## A suspect bug in a Lincoln listing

Nebraska Extension in Lancaster County says the first step with a suspect insect is a professional identification. Its local number is the reason: about half of the suspected bed bugs brought to the Lancaster County office end up being bat bugs. For a Lincoln home, the same page says a bat bug problem is solved by removing bats roosting in or around the home, and that insecticide in the bedroom is not normally needed once the bats are gone. A Lincoln host who skips identification can end up treating the wrong problem.

For a confirmed bed bug, the Extension page recommends a pest management professional, says the majority of treatments need at least two visits, and suggests watching for 6 to 8 months afterward. Lancaster County Extension's mattress inspection advice: seams, folds and zippers, looking for live bugs, shed skins and black fecal spots. Sleep Sanitation explains how [bed bugs differ from dust mites and bed mites (house dust mites)](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-dust-mites-allergens/dust-mites-vs-bed-bugs-vs-bed-mites). This page is general information, not medical advice.

## Where our cleaning fits for Lincoln hosts

Airbnb asks Lincoln hosts for a clean listing without saying how. What we document at a Lincoln listing is the visit: an inspection form covering the mattress material, special care notes and any urine or odor observations. Our only tests at a Lincoln listing are a moisture test after the job, a bedroom CO2 test and a built-in dust mite sensor on the UV-C vacuum. Technicians wear gloves and shoe booties, and equipment is disinfected between Lincoln jobs. A Lincoln mattress in an active bed bug case waits until the pest professional is done. Check the care label and warranty terms first.
