## What kind of homes are downtown and in the Haymarket?

Almost all of them are in larger buildings. In the downtown tract, 90% of homes are in buildings of ten or more units and 1% are detached houses; citywide those figures are 23% and 55%. Studios are 17% of downtown homes, against 3% across Lincoln. Yet 17% of downtown homes have four bedrooms, the same share as the city, which in a tract with almost no houses means large apartment units.

The Haymarket and West Haymarket tract is the newest part of the core: 41% of its homes were built in the 2010s, the decade Pinnacle Bank Arena opened in West Haymarket. The other end of the age range is the historic district itself. Its National Register nomination describes eight blocks of the 1867 Original Plat where warehouses are the predominant building type, from 1880s brick grocery warehouses to the 1927 Burlington Depot, with offices and apartments on several upper floors.

## How does a mattress visit work in a building?

Logistics first. Tell us the building name, entrance, unit and floor, and whether your building wants contractors on a service elevator or signed in at the desk. The mattress is cleaned where it sits, so it never has to fit in an elevator. The visit itself is the same as anywhere in Lincoln: a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment. The method is explained in the [Sleep Sanitation Knowledge Center](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center).

## Is there anything different about a loft or a new tower?

Air, mostly. The Haymarket's warehouses were built with large windows, and a sleeping area by a sunny window runs warm in a Lincoln summer. Lincoln Airport's average July dew point is about 65°F, and LLCHD recommends 30 to 50 percent humidity indoors; our [Lincoln humidity and bed mites guide](/allergy-season/lincoln-humidity-and-bed-mites) has the details. In any of them, the bed stays unmade until it is dry to the touch, and we run a moisture check after the job.
