## What kind of homes does Wilderness Hills have?

Newer, family-sized houses plus a cluster of apartments. In the census tract that covers Wilderness Hills, the median home was built in 2000, nothing dates from before 1960, and 68% of homes are detached houses. Homes are large: 80% have three or more bedrooms, and only 12% have two, against 28% across Lincoln. The Aventine at Wilderness Hills apartments, 220 units off 30th and Yankee Hill, broke ground in 2013.

## What does that mean for a mattress visit?

Usually a full house of beds: a main suite, kids' rooms and a guest room, often in one visit at the lower additional-mattress rates. We clean each mattress where it sits. Every visit is a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, plus two checks: a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.
