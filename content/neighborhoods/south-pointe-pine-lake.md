## What kind of homes are around South Pointe?

Newer ones, and a real mix. SouthPointe Pavilions opened at 27th and Pine Lake in fall 1998, and the neighborhood grew with it. In the census tract around the center, the median home was built in 1995 and almost nothing dates from before 1960. Homes split evenly: 46% detached houses, 46% apartments in buildings of five units or more. Two-bedroom homes are the most common size, at 35% against 28% citywide.

## What does that mean for a mattress visit?

Often one or two mattresses in an apartment, or a full house of beds in a family home a few streets over. We clean each mattress where it sits. Every visit is a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, plus two checks: a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.
