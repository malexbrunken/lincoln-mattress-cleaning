## What kind of homes does Fallbrook have?

New, and often big. Fallbrook was planned from 1999 as a walkable mixed-use neighborhood, with a town center, boulevards and trails, and most of it went up after 2000. The two census tracts that cover it have median homes built in 2014 and 2008, against 1982 citywide, and four-bedroom homes make up 35% and 29% of them, against 17% across Lincoln.

## What does that mean for a mattress visit?

More beds per house. A Fallbrook visit is often a main suite, two or three kids' rooms and a guest bed, and that's where the lower additional-mattress rates add up. We clean each mattress where it sits: a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, plus a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.

## New house, new dust

Newer homes and nearby construction mean fine dust in the first years.
